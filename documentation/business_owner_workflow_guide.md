### Step 1: The Employee Submits a Leave Request
* **What the Employee Does**:
  1. The employee opens the self-service web portal on their smartphone or computer.
  2. They immediately see their **real-time available balances** (e.g. *Annual: 11 days | Sick: 8 days | Casual: 5 days*).
  3. They click **"Apply for Leave"**, select the leave category (e.g., Annual Vacation), and tap the dates on an interactive calendar.
  4. The system prompts them to **nominate an eligible colleague** to cover their shift responsibilities.
  5. The employee enters a brief note and taps **"Submit"**.
* **What the System Does Automatically Behind the Scenes**:
  - **Notice Check**: Verifies whether the request meets the company's advance notice policy (e.g., 3 days in advance for vacation).
  - **Balance Lock**: Temporarily moves the requested days from *Available* to *Reserved / Pending* so the employee cannot book more leave than they own.
  - **Coverage Filter**: Only allows the employee to nominate peers who work in the **same branch**, share the **same job role**, and are **not already on leave** that day.

---

### Step 2: The Shift Peer Accepts Coverage Duty
In any customer-facing, retail, or shift-based business, no employee should leave an empty chair or unmanned machine.
* **What the Nominated Peer Does**:
  1. The nominated colleague receives an alert on their portal: *"Kasun has requested you to cover his Morning Shift on Thursday, Oct 18."*
  2. The colleague opens their **Coverage Requests** tab.
  3. They see the exact date, shift hours, and handover notes.
  4. They tap **"Agree & Accept Shift"** (or **"Decline"** if genuinely unavailable).
* **What the System Does Automatically Behind the Scenes**:
  - **Workplace Safety & Fatigue Check**: The system checks the colleague’s own roster. If taking this shift would give them less than **11 hours of rest** between shifts, the system blocks the handover to prevent exhausted staff and workplace accidents.
  - **What if the Colleague Declines?**: The application **never goes to the manager**. The applicant is instantly notified to choose another colleague. Your branch manager's time is never wasted reviewing requests that lack operational coverage.

---

### Step 3: The Branch Manager Reviews with the "Capacity Meter"
Once coverage is agreed upon, the application arrives on the Branch Manager's dashboard.
* **What the Manager Sees**:
  The manager does not have to guess if the branch has enough staff. The platform provides a live **Team Capacity Meter**:
  ```
  Branch: Kandy Retail Showroom
  Date Requested: Friday, October 19 (Morning Shift)
  Total Team Size: 10 Staff
  Already on Approved Leave: 2 Staff
  This Request (Kasun): 1 Staff
  Remaining Active Floor Staff: 7 Staff (70% Floor Capacity)
  Branch Safety Threshold: MINIMUM 6 STAFF REQUIRED [STATUS: HEALTHY - GREEN]
  ```
* **The Manager’s Decision**:
  - **To Approve**: The manager clicks **"Approve"**. Kasun gets an instant confirmation on his phone, and the shift is officially locked.
  - **To Reject**: The manager clicks **"Reject"** and **must enter a business reason** (e.g., *"Major promotional sale scheduled on this date; all hands required on the sales floor"*).
  - **Instant Quota Refund**: When rejected, the reserved leave days are **immediately credited back to Kasun’s balance**.

---

### Step 4: Schedules Update Automatically
Once approved:
1. The **Monthly Branch Shift Roster** automatically updates:
   - Kasun is marked as `ON APPROVED LEAVE`.
   - The substitute is officially scheduled as `DUTY COVER`.
2. The Branch Master Calendar highlights the absence so supervisors know exactly who is in the building at all times.
3. Zero manual updates to whiteboards, Excel sheets, or paper rotas.

---

### Step 5: Month-End Payroll Synchronization (Zero Leaks)
On the 25th of every month, your Finance & Payroll team processes salary slips.
* **What the Payroll Accountant Does**:
  1. The accountant opens **Payroll Reports > Monthly Loss of Pay (Unpaid Leave) Summary**.
  2. They select the billing cutoff (e.g. Aug 26 to Sep 25).
  3. The system generates a clean, audit-ready breakdown:
     - Employee Name & ID
     - Branch Location
     - Exact number of **Unpaid Days** taken
     - Specific calendar dates of absence
  4. The accountant clicks **"Export to Excel / CSV"** and imports the deductions straight into the payroll system.
* **The Financial Result**: The company **never accidentally pays a full month's salary** to an employee who took unpaid time off. Every single unpaid day is captured and deducted with 100% accuracy.

## 3. How Real-World Special Scenarios Are Handled

Businesses face messy, real-world situations every week. Here is how the system handles each one:

---

### Scenario A: Emergency Morning Illness (Sick Leave)
* **The Problem**: An employee wakes up with high fever at 6:30 AM and cannot give 3 days advance notice.
* **The System Workflow**:
  1. The employee selects `Sick / Medical Leave`.
  2. The system recognizes that medical emergencies cannot be planned in advance and **automatically relaxes the 3-day notice rule**.
  3. The supervisor receives an immediate notification: *"EMERGENCY ABSENCE: Kamal Silva is absent today due to illness."*
  4. If the employee is sick for **more than 2 consecutive days**, the system locks the request until the employee snaps a photo of their **Doctor's Medical Certificate** using their smartphone camera and uploads it directly.

---

### Scenario B: Half-Day Leave
* **The Problem**: An employee needs to attend a bank appointment or parent-teacher meeting in the afternoon.
* **The System Workflow**:
  1. The employee toggles **"Half Day"** on the application form.
  2. They choose either **Morning Half** (first 4 hours) or **Afternoon Half** (second 4 hours).
  3. The system deducts exactly **0.5 days** (half a day) from their leave quota.
  4. The branch roster shows they are on duty for the other half of their shift.

---

### Scenario C: Factory / Night Shift Workers (Crosses Midnight)
* **The Problem**: A factory worker on the Night Shift works from 10:00 PM Friday night to 06:00 AM Saturday morning. If they fall ill, basic computer spreadsheets mistakenly deduct 2 full days of leave because two dates are touched.
* **The System Workflow**:
  1. The system understands **Shift Masters**.
  2. It recognizes that a night shift is **one single operational shift unit**.
  3. Exactly **1 shift of sick leave** is deducted, keeping shift rosters and payroll completely accurate.

---

### Scenario D: Leaves Spanning Weekends & Public Holidays
* **The Problem**: An office worker takes leave from Thursday through Tuesday (6 calendar days). Saturday and Sunday are normal weekend days, and Monday is a statutory Poya / Mercantile public holiday.
* **The System Workflow**:
  1. The employee selects the 6 calendar days.
  2. The system checks the branch holiday calendar and operating schedule:
     - Weekend Days: **-2 Days (Free)**
     - Public Holiday: **-1 Day (Free)**
     - Net Deducted Working Days: **3 Days (Thursday, Friday, Tuesday)**.
  3. The employee is never unfairly charged for days the company was closed anyway.

---

### Scenario E: What Happens on Scheduled Rest Days (RDO)?
* **The Problem**: A retail associate whose scheduled rest days this week are Tuesday and Wednesday accidentally includes Tuesday in their leave request.
* **The System Workflow**:
  1. The system checks their monthly published roster.
  2. It detects Tuesday is a **Rostered Day Off (RDO)**.
  3. The system alerts the employee: *"Tuesday is your scheduled rest day. Deducting 0 days."*
  4. The employee's hard-earned leave balance is preserved.

---

### Scenario F: What If an Employee Runs Out of Leave?
* **The Problem**: An employee has used all their annual and casual leave, but asks for 2 days off for personal reasons.
* **The System Workflow**:
  1. The employee attempts to book Annual Leave.
  2. The system displays: `Insufficient balance (0 days available)`.
  3. The only available option is **Loss of Pay (Unpaid Leave)**.
  4. The system presents a clear financial warning:
     > *"Warning: You have 0 paid days remaining. This request is for Unpaid Leave. 2 days of salary will be deducted from your month-end payslip."*
  5. If approved by management, the 2 days are sent straight to the month-end payroll deduction file.

---

### Scenario G: Changing Plans or Emergency Recall
* **Cancelling Before Approval**: The employee clicks "Cancel" in their portal; reserved days are instantly returned to their balance.
* **Cancelling After Approval**: Because staffing schedules have already been arranged, an approved cancellation requires the Branch Manager's sign-off to ensure the shift roster can be adjusted back.
* **Emergency Management Recall**: If a factory machine breaks down and management urgently calls an on-leave technician back to duty, management clicks **"Emergency Recall"**. The unused leave days are **immediately refunded back to the technician's balance**.

### 4.1 How to Create and Manage Leave Types
1. **Navigate to the Menu**: The HR Administrator opens **Admin Console > Leave Configuration > Manage Leave Types**.
2. **Click "Create Leave Type"**:
   - **Name**: e.g., `Maternity Leave`, `Study / Exam Leave`, `Bereavement Leave`.
   - **System Code**: Automatically generated (e.g., `maternity_leave`, `study_leave`).
   - **Type Nature**: Toggle **Paid** (salary maintained) or **Unpaid / Loss of Pay** (deducted from salary).
   - **Color Badge**: Select a color tag (e.g., Purple for Annual, Emerald for Sick, Blue for Casual) to visually highlight this category across team calendars.
3. **Configure Company Day Counts & Policy Rules**:
   - **Advance Notice Days**: Input how many days in advance this leave must be booked (e.g., `3` days for Annual, `0` days for Sick, `1` day for Casual).
   - **Max Consecutive Days**: Restrict the maximum continuous days allowed in one application (e.g., `2` days for Casual leave, or `0` for unlimited).
   - **Medical Certificate Threshold**: Set the number of consecutive days that trigger mandatory medical certificate upload (e.g., `2` days for Sick Leave).
4. **Save**: The Leave Type is immediately active across the system.

---

   - **Description**: e.g., *"Applies to confirmed permanent factory operators in Galle and Kandy"*.
3. **Allocate Exact Quota Days for Every Leave Type**:
   The screen displays every active leave type in your company with an easy number input:
   ```

   ┌──────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┐

   │ Sick / Medical Leave             │ [ 14 ] Days                 │
   │ Casual / Personal Leave          │ [  7 ] Days                 │

   │ Loss of Pay (Unpaid)             │ [ 30 ] Days Max             │
   └──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┘
   ```
4. **Dynamic Sync Intelligence**:
   If the company later creates a brand new leave type (e.g. *Paternity Leave*), the system **automatically injects it into all existing Leave Profiles** with a `New Leave Type` badge and sets default days to `0` until HR edits it.

### 4.3 Assigning Employees to Leave Profiles

### 5.1 How to Define Shift Masters
1. **Navigate to the Menu**: Open **Admin Console > Shift Management > Shift Masters**.
2. **Click "Add New Shift"**:
   - **Branch**: Select which branch facility operates this shift (e.g., Galle Factory).
   - **Shift Code & Name**: e.g., `M-01` (Morning Shift), `E-01` (Evening Shift), `N-01` (Night Shift).
   - **Start Time & End Time**: e.g., `06:00` to `14:00` (Morning), `14:00` to `22:00` (Evening), `22:00` to `06:00` (Night).
   - **Crosses Midnight Flag**: If the shift begins on one date and finishes the next morning (e.g. 10 PM to 6 AM), toggle **"Crosses Midnight" = ON**.
   - **Operational Duration**: e.g., `8.00` Hours.
3. **Save**: The shift is now available to be scheduled in that branch.

---

### 5.2 How Supervisors Publish the Monthly Shift Roster
1. **Navigate to the Menu**: The Shift Supervisor opens **Admin Console > Shift Management > Monthly Roster Grid**.
2. **Select Branch & Month**: e.g., *Galle Manufacturing Plant — October 2026*.

4. **Quick Assignment**:
   - The supervisor clicks any cell to toggle shifts or mark a **Rostered Day Off (RDO)**.
   - Supervisors can also use **"Apply 3-Shift Rotating Pattern"** to automatically schedule rotating teams with 1 click.
5. **Publish**: Once published, employees see their personalized working schedule directly on their mobile portal.

---

### 5.3 How the System Uses the Roster During Leave Applications
* **Night Shift Accuracy**: When a worker on the `22:00 – 06:00` Night Shift requests leave, the system knows this is **1 shift unit**. It deducts exactly 1 day of leave (never 2 calendar days).
* **Rostered Day Off (RDO) Protection**: If an employee takes leave spanning Wednesday (their scheduled RDO), the system recognizes the RDO and deducts **0 days**.
* **Half-Day Shifts**: If an employee takes half-day leave on an 8-hour shift, the system deducts **0.5 shift units** (4 hours) and marks them available for the remaining 4 hours.

---

### 5.4 The 11-Hour Rest Interval Safety Guard (Fatigue Protection)
* **The Legal & Safety Problem**: An employee asks colleague Sunimal to cover their Morning Shift starting at 06:00 AM on Wednesday. However, Sunimal’s roster shows he worked until 10:00 PM on Tuesday night. Between 10 PM and 6 AM is only **8 hours of rest**. In heavy manufacturing, transport, or medical wards, a fatigued worker is a major accident and legal liability.
* **The System Action**:
  - The platform checks Sunimal’s roster for the preceding 24 hours.
  - It detects that the rest gap is under **11 hours**.
  - The system **automatically blocks Sunimal from being selected**, displaying:
    > *"Unavailable: Sunimal worked until 22:00 yesterday. Selecting him violates the mandatory 11-hour rest interval."*
* **Business Outcome**: Zero workplace accidents due to shift handover fatigue. Complete compliance with occupational safety and labor laws.

## 6. Master Functional Guide: Branch & Holiday Calendar Configuration

Your business operates across multiple physical locations within Sri Lanka (e.g. Head Office in Colombo, Retail Flagship in Kandy, Manufacturing Plant in Galle). This section explains **how each domestic branch is configured independently**.

---

### 6.1 Configuring Branch Operating Schedules
1. Open **Admin Console > Organization > Manage Branches**.
2. Select the branch and configure its **Operating Model**:
   - **Corporate 5-Day (Head Office, Colombo)**: Working Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday and Sunday are marked as non-working weekends.
   - **Retail 6-Day (Kandy Showroom)**: Working Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday is marked as non-working.
   - **Continuous 24/7 (Galle Plant)**: Operates 7 days a week, 24 hours a day on 3 rotating shifts.
3. **Business Outcome**: An employee applying for leave in Colombo over a Saturday is charged 0 days. An employee in Kandy applying for Saturday is charged 1 working day.

---

### 6.2 Managing Branch Public & Mercantile Holidays
1. Open **Admin Console > Organization > Branch Holiday Calendars**.
2. Select the branch location:
   - For **Colombo Head Office**: Add national Bank and Mercantile Holidays.
   - For **Galle Factory**: Add statutory Poya holidays, but keep commercial bank holidays marked as open working days per factory collective agreements.
3. **Automated Holiday Exclusion**:
   When staff apply for leave spanning any declared holiday for their branch, the system automatically subtracts the holiday from the leave deduction. Staff are never charged leave for official closed holidays.
